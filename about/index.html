About us

Medical AI research team

We are a research team focused on developing AI methods and tools that can support healthcare professionals and improve patient care.

Our approach

We combine rigorous methods with close collaboration with clinicians and institutions.

  • Evidence-based

    Our work is grounded in medical evidence and validated on appropriate datasets. We publish our methods and results to support reproducibility.

  • Interdisciplinary

    We work across AI, medicine, and health informatics. Collaboration with clinicians and domain experts is central to our research.

  • Responsible innovation

    We care about safety, fairness, and transparency. Our goal is technology that supports—not replaces—clinical judgment.

What we value

These principles guide how we conduct research and build tools.

Rigor

Sound methodology, clear evaluation, and honest reporting of limitations.

Openness

We share code, data where possible, and findings to advance the field.

Impact

We aim for work that can meaningfully support healthcare delivery and research.
